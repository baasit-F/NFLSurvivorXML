Read this before trusting a number

The market is not beaten. Boosted trees over ~40 features lose to the closing spread on both log-loss and Brier across a 15-season walk-forward. Blending was tested at every weight and zero was optimal, so where a line exists this board simply reports the market. The modelling exists for the games the books have not priced.

Weeks 4–6 are the least reliable. The ratings model scores Brier 0.2345 there against 0.2135 in weeks 11–14 — early on, Elo is still mostly carrying last season forward and over-reacting to two or three games of this one. Those cells deserve the least confidence, which is the reverse of what their stated probabilities suggest.

Distant weeks are shrunk toward a coin flip before the plan is solved, because every historical game was predicted from the doorstep with the injury report known. Nothing in the training data can teach a model how much less it knows in September about week 15.
